Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

This policy applies to every mobile game published by Nanno Studios (“we”, “us”) on Google Play, including Brik Puzzle, Whack A Mole: Home Rescue and any future titles. It explains what data is handled when you play our games.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no personal information is asked of you.
  • Nothing is stored on our servers — your progress and settings live only on your device.
  • The only data processed is by Google AdMob, to show ads that keep the games free.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games are supported by ads served through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID / device identifier
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions and clicks)

Google explains how it uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partners. You can opt out of ad personalisation in your device under Settings → Google → Ads, or at adssettings.google.com.

Your consent choices (EEA & UK)

When required by law, a consent form is shown the first time the game opens, letting you choose whether personalised ads may be served. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time through the Privacy options entry in the game’s settings menu.

Permissions the game requests

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and detect when you are offline.
  • Vibrate — for touch feedback.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve ads.

Children

Our games are general-audience entertainment and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Because game progress never leaves your device, uninstalling the game removes everything associated with it. There is no server copy for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our games change. The effective date above will always show when it was last revised.
