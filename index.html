Brik Puzzle

A colour-gate block puzzle that never runs out. Slide every brik out through a gate of its own colour. The wrong colour refuses it with a buzz, the right one snaps it out and shatters it.

  • Endless levels: every board is generated for you, and every one is solvable
  • Six worlds with their own look, and a star toll guarding each one
  • Par worked out by a real solver. Three stars means you matched the machine
  • Shards, shockwaves, spoken call-outs and haptics

Whack A Mole: Home Rescue

The moles got in. They popped up through the floors, wrecked the furniture and made themselves at home. Grab the mallet and take Grandpa's house back, room by room.

  • Helmet, goggle, magician and crown moles, each with their own tricks
  • Watch the bombs: whack a grey mole and it costs you a heart
  • Clear levels to earn furniture shards and rebuild every room
  • Earn keys to unlock eight rooms of a rambling old house

About the studio

Built small. Played big.

Nanno Studios is an independent game studio making games for phones. We care about tight controls, satisfying feedback and respecting your time.

Endless by design

Our levels are generated, not hand-placed, so the game never runs out.

Juice in every tap

Shards, shockwaves, haptics and sound. Every action should feel good.

No accounts

No sign-ups and no personal info. Your progress lives on your device.

Free to play

Our games are free, supported by optional ads that never block your fun.
